## The story
My own childhood in an Addis Ababa sefer (neighbourhood). I told it in two Amharic voice notes on 2026-11-26 (61 s total, kept out of git).
Gemini transcribed and translated them (`story/transcript.json`); I checked the transcript and fixed one mistranslation ("children who play
and children who play" should be "children who played and children who didn't"). The core: we played with the neighbours' children all day; only one
house had a TV, and we all gathered there to watch the drama *Gemena* and talked about it afterwards. I look back with joy, not regret,
and learned that children need to play, because children who never played find it hard to get along with people.

## Audience
Ethiopians aged 18–35, in Ethiopia and the diaspora, who grew up in a sefer in the 2000s–2010s, plus young parents raising
children among screens. They watch on phones (TikTok, Telegram, Facebook), so the 9:16 cut is the main one, with sound often low,
which is why every line has a burned-in English subtitle. They think in Amharic, so the narration is Amharic. They already know *Gemena* and the
one-TV-house ritual. That recognition is the hook, so the film never explains it.

## Intent
**Feel:** the warmth of a shared childhood they recognise as their own.
**Understand:** playing together is how children learn to get along with people. The TV mattered because it gathered us, not because of the screen.
**Do:** send their children or younger siblings outside to play, or message a childhood friend from the sefer.

## Why each shot exists
Each shot's `purpose` field in storyboard.json is the short version. Structure: place (s01) → play (s02–s03) → the one TV (s04) →
the gathering (s05) → a silent beat inside the feeling (s06, no narration on purpose) → talking afterwards (s07) → today, joy not regret (s08) →
the lesson as a contrast image (s09: a child alone with a tablet while others play outside) → the call to action, echoing s02 (s10).
Added by me beyond the voice notes: the skipping rope and hide-and-seek images (the notes say only "we played a lot"); the present-day contrast in s09.
